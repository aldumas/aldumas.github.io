Refund policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Tips are voluntary and nothing is sold in exchange for them, but mistakes happen, and I'd rather you were happy than out of pocket.

Refunds

  • If you tipped by mistake, tipped the wrong amount, or simply changed your mind, email contact@misterinevitable.com within 30 days of the payment and I'll refund it in full.
  • Include the date, the amount and the name or email you used on Ko-fi.
  • Refunds go back to the original payment method. Once I've issued one, it usually reaches you within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank.
  • After 30 days I'll still look at any request, especially one about an unauthorised payment.

Monthly tips

You can cancel a monthly tip on Ko-fi at any time, which stops future payments. If a renewal you didn't expect went through, email me within 30 days and I'll refund it.

Unauthorised payments

If you see a payment to me you didn't make, please email me first: I'll refund it straight away. You can also contact your card issuer.
