Privacy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This website

This site has no accounts, no cookies of its own, no analytics and no ads. It's hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security; see GitHub's privacy statement.

The Support page embeds Ko-fi's tip form. When you open that page, your browser loads the form directly from Ko-fi, which may set its own cookies and receive your IP address; Ko-fi's privacy policy covers this. No other page loads anything from Ko-fi.

Tips

When you tip on Ko-fi, Ko-fi and its payment processors (Stripe, and PayPal if you choose it) handle your payment details. I never receive your card number. Ko-fi shares with me what it shows creators: typically your name or display name, email address, the amount and any message you leave. I use these only to thank you, answer you and handle refunds or payment problems. I don't sell or share them.

Email

If you email me, I keep the conversation so I can reply and follow up.

The mods

My mods don't collect personal data or send anything over the internet. Night Warden runs on your own server or game and stores its settings and state in your world's files.

Your choices

To ask what I hold about you or to have it deleted (apart from records I must keep for tax or payment purposes), email contact@misterinevitable.com.
