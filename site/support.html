What a tip is

  • A tip is a voluntary contribution. It doesn't buy a product, a service, early access, in-game items or any other benefit, and the mods work exactly the same whether you tip or not.
  • Tips pay for my time developing, testing and supporting the mods.
  • Amounts are shown in the currency you choose on Ko-fi before you pay, and you confirm the amount before anything is charged.
  • A monthly tip renews each month until you cancel it on Ko-fi, which you can do at any time. Cancelling stops future payments.
