Minecraft mods by Adam Dumas

More stories for your Minecraft world.

I make mods for Minecraft: Java Edition. I'm drawn to side quests and the moments players tell each other about afterwards. The first, Night Warden, is in development.

What I do

I'm Adam Dumas, a software developer who makes mods for Minecraft: Java Edition in my spare time. For now it's a hobby and an experiment. I design, build, test and support the mods myself.

Disability is part of my family's everyday life, and I care about people understanding it better. Where it fits, I'd like my work to help with that.

In development

Night Warden

A Fabric mod for servers, singleplayer and LAN worlds. On some nights a Warden emerges near one marked player, hunts them until dawn, and then digs back underground. Only the server, or the player hosting the world, needs to install it. Everyone else can join without it.
