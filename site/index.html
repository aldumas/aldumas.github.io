Minecraft mods by Adam Dumas

More stories for your Minecraft world.

I make mods for Minecraft: Java Edition. I'm drawn to side quests and the moments players tell each other about afterwards. The first, Night Warden, is in development.

What I do

I'm Adam Dumas, a software developer who makes mods for Minecraft: Java Edition. This is my one-person business, run under my own name. I design, build, test and support the mods myself.

Disability is part of my family's everyday life, and I care about people understanding it better. Where it fits, I'd like my work to help with that.

In development

Night Warden

A server-side Fabric mod. On some nights a Warden emerges near one marked player, hunts them until dawn, and then digs back underground. Players don't need to install anything: vanilla clients can join.

How payments work

The mods are free to download and use. I fund the work through voluntary tips from players and server owners who enjoy them (see Support).

  • What you pay for: nothing is sold. Tips are voluntary contributions to support the development of free Minecraft mods.
  • Where: tips are taken on my Ko-fi page, ko-fi.com/misterinevitable. Card payments are processed by Stripe.
  • Price: you choose the amount. There are no subscriptions unless you set one up on Ko-fi yourself, and you can cancel it there at any time.
  • Questions or refunds: email contact@misterinevitable.com. See the refund policy.
