# Parent Article Ideas

_Status: `LIVING`. Created 2026-10-02. Last updated: 2026-10-02._

Ideas for this blog, written from the parent's side rather than the
product's. Most come from `../microsaas`, where they were collected as
product arguments, marketing pieces or personal notes. Each row names its
source so the argument doesn't have to be rebuilt. Paths are relative to
`../microsaas/docs/`.

**Reader** says who the post is mainly for:
**F** = friends, family, church, the people around us.
**P** = other parents. ⚠️ This reader triggers `../rules-ledger.md` §2c:
no preaching about their own life.
**O** = an onlooker who searched something at 11pm.

**Gate** is anything that must be true before it's published. Every post
about her waits on `../rules-ledger.md` §7 (her name, the withdrawal
promise, my wife's yes). That gate is assumed for every row and not
repeated.
